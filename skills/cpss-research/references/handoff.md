# Research Handoff Record

Use this structure flexibly. Include only sections that are relevant, but preserve all material evidence needed by a later validator or reviewer.

## Research question
State the question, idea, premise, or decision being researched.

## Scope
Record important boundaries: timeframe, geography, population, product/version, definitions, exclusions, and user-supplied constraints.

## Bottom line
State the current evidence-based conclusion without overstating confidence.

## Key findings
For each material finding include:
- claim or finding;
- supporting evidence and source provenance;
- relevant dates/freshness;
- material quantitative details, definitions, or baselines;
- confidence or important caveat.

## Contradictory or limiting evidence
Record credible conflicts, negative findings, methodological limitations, or evidence that narrows the conclusion.

## Assumptions and inferences
Separate user-provided assumptions from analyst inference. Do not promote either to verified fact.

## Unresolved questions
List gaps that could materially change the conclusion or deserve later validation.

## Source record
List the material sources used, enough to re-open or identify them later. Note source type and any access limitation when relevant.

## Handoff note
State what a downstream validator should test most carefully, including weak claims, disputed evidence, causal interpretations, freshness-sensitive claims, or source-quality concerns.
